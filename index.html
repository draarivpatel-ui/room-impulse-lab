    What the model knows, and what it ignores.

    Statistical reverberation

    Sabine: T = 0.161 V / A. Eyring: T = 0.161 V / (-S ln(1 - a_bar)). Millington-Sette sums -S_i ln(1 - a_i) per surface. Air absorption adds 4 m V at the top bands.

    Image sources

    Each wall mirror is an integer lattice cell. Delay is distance over 343 m/s; pressure gain is 1/distance times sqrt(1 - alpha) per bounce. Order caps at 6.

    Decay analysis

    Schroeder backward integration turns the impulse response into an energy decay curve. EDT fits 0 to -10 dB, T20 fits -5 to -25, T30 fits -5 to -35 and extrapolates to 60 dB.

    Limits

    Geometric acoustics: no diffraction, scattering, flutter echo modes or coupled rooms. The diffuse tail is seeded noise matched to Eyring decay, a teaching stand-in for a measured room.

    Honesty note. Every number is computed live from the room you describe. Nothing is recorded, uploaded or measured from a real space. Measured T30 comes from the same synthesized response the charts show, so any gap between model and measurement is the model's own limitation, not hidden.
